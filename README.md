# rOtOr ⚛️
### Dual-Vortex Logarithmic Manifold & Harmonic Reduction Engine

It calculates generalized higher-order Fibonacci recurrences, maps their digital root flux states ($1 \to 2 \to 4 \to 8 \to 7 \to 5$) and Pisano periods, and projects them into counter-rotating dual logarithmic vortices embedded inside a parametric Crea-Torus field.

## ⚡ Mathematical Mechanics

### 1. Higher-Order Recurrence ($k$-Step)
Given an order $k \ge 2$, the sequence is calculated recursively:
$$F_n^{(k)} = \sum_{j=1}^{k} F_{n-j}^{(k)}$$
Seeds: $F_0^{(k)} = 0, \dots, F_{k-2}^{(k)} = 0, F_{k-1}^{(k)} = 1$.

### 2. Dual Counter-Rotating Vortex Dynamics
The continuous trajectory is driven by a logarithmic growth engine:
$$r(t) = a \cdot e^{b \cdot t}, \quad b = \frac{\ln \phi}{2\pi}$$
- **Expanding Vortex (+ / Yang)**: Parametrized along $(+t)$
- **Converging Vortex (- / Yin)**: Counter-rotated along $(-t)$ with inverted vertical axis $(-z)$

### 3. Modulo-9 Vortex Math & Residue Periods
- **Digital Root Vectors**: Automated recursive reduction to mod 9 mapping the harmonic flux matrix.
- **Pisano Harmonics & Binary Parity**: Real-time modular frequency mapping.
